# corpus

Synthetic corpora only: generated, mutated, and (later) public historical
transactions. Every divergence ever found lives here as a witness with the rule
it exercises. Nothing from the private programs (`tools/mechanism_check.sh`).

`INDEX.md` is the index (the funnel rule): every corpus and every witness, its
sha, the implementations whose verdicts it records, and the rule vocabulary.
`seed/` is the seed corpus (#4): 10,000 entries `[X]` with the spec's, bsv-rs 0.3.28's (the pin since 2026-09-24), both SDKs' and SV-node's verdicts recorded (`INDEX.md` §1), regenerated by `make seed` and re-recorded by the differentials (`make diff`, `make enginesdiff`, `run_svnode --record`).
