## The seven diverges

[M] Four are the reject-family trace rows the brief named; all four are **harness errors**, not Findings, with one root cause.

- `mempool/rejects-silence-until-reset` (step 1), `mempool/rejects-lazy-reset-tip-returns` (step 1), `mempool/rejects-commit-refusal-not-remembered` (step 3) and `mempool/rejects-rpc-neither-reads-nor-writes` (steps 0, 1, 3, 5): in every diverging step the reference's token is `rejected 16 bad-txns-in-belowout` (code 16, the sum of outputs exceeds the sum of inputs) and the node accepted the transaction. The cause is in the trace road's funding, not the node. `harness/src/scenarios/road.rs` `trace_row` funds each declared coin with a fresh 100,000-satoshi OP_TRUE coin from `road.coin()`, not with the scenario's own prevout value; the `bad-txns-in-belowout` verdict depends on the input being smaller than the output, so the remap destroys it. The frame `T` of `rejects-silence-until-reset` pays 1,001 satoshis (`e903...` in its serialized output) on a remapped 100,000-satoshi input, so the node correctly accepts a well-funded transaction while the reference's recorded token rejected the original small-input frame. The four rows are owed a funding that preserves the scenario's prevout value on the trace road (the admission road already preserves values per `harness/engines/instance41/README.md`); until then they are harness errors and the second oracle's answer on them is not the rule. These diverges are the projection's `judged` set on a `tx` or `rpc` step whose class moved from refused to accepted (`harness/src/scenarios/road.rs` `trace_row`): the projection is correct; the inputs it fed the node were not.

The other three are measured block and chain diverges, outside the four the brief named, recorded here and owed separate adjudication (each may be a node divergence or a mapping artifact of the road's value or time remap, to be read against the projection before it is called either way):

- `block/context/nonfinal-at-median` and `block/header/time-at-median`: the reference rejects at the median-time boundary; the node connected the block onto the active tip (`ok`). The road sets each block's time above the parent's median time past; the row needs the time exactly at the median, which the road's time selection may not reproduce (a candidate mapping artifact, not yet confirmed).
- `chain/chain-invalidate-reconsider`: the RPC chain row (`harness/src/scenarios/road.rs` chain rows) read a snapshot after invalidate and reconsider that differed from the reference's; it reproduced the partial run's diverge and is owed a read against the snapshot projection.
