## Finding

On cut 2's road the block family is delivered to the instance through the legacy P2P
bridge rather than over RPC. The road builds each block row's body and sends the block
to the bridge's one loopback peer, then reads the fate (active on the tip, a reject
naming it, or no answer within twenty seconds): `harness/src/scenarios/road.rs`
`deliver` and `structural`. The six rows that build on a populated body were measured
and answered (`block/valid` active, `block/pow-before-root` refused, `block/root-mismatch`
and two duplicate rows read, `block/duplicate-interior` refused). The next row,
`block/empty`, assembles a block that carries no transactions at all, not even a
coinbase, and sends it over the bridge. The node did not answer that row with a verdict:
its process ended instead, on an out-of-range index inside the legacy sync manager's
block handler, at `services/legacy/netsync/handle_block.go:205` reached from the block
handler of `services/legacy/netsync/manager.go` (the handler reads the body's coinbase
transaction before it checks that the body holds any transaction, so a zero-length body
indexes an empty list). The process end took down every service of the instance, so the
road's next RPC call found the loopback port closed and the run stopped with a non-zero
code, leaving the remaining block, context-block, header, pow, reward and ttor rows of
the block family, and whatever the column would have measured after them, unmeasured.

This is the instance's own behavior, not a setting of the recipe or a defect of the
harness: the recipe of record (the bridge off) never reaches this path, and over RPC
`submitblock` is unimplemented at the pin (`services/rpc/Server.go:211`,
`harness/engines/instance41/README.md`), so the empty-body block has no RPC surface that
could have exercised the same read. A correct node rejects a block whose body holds no
coinbase; this instance, asked the question over the legacy bridge, ends its process
instead of rejecting the block. The reference (`bitcoin-sv` at the pin of
`docs/PROVENANCE.md`) rejects an empty-body block (`bad-cb-missing`), so the row's cell
is a hard divergence: a process end against a rejection. The harness cannot carry the
row to a comparable verdict on this road without ending the node, so the row is withheld
and classed `unrealizable` on cut 2's road, with this Finding as its reason
(`harness/src/scenarios/road.rs` `block_reason`, the `block/empty` arm). The row stays
owed a verdict until the instance is fixed upstream or a non-bridge surface for an
empty-body block exists.

## What changed in the harness

`harness/src/scenarios/road.rs`: `block/empty` is removed from `BLOCK_ROWS` (its delivery
recipe in `structural` with it), and a `block_reason` arm classes it `unrealizable` on cut
2's road, citing `services/legacy/netsync/handle_block.go:205` and this record. The rest
of the block family is delivered and measured after the change.
