## 5. The proposal for the campaign's next cut

The campaign today: one column (the spec against the pinned node), one generator (`seed_gen`'s families arith,
nonmin, size, depth, num2bin and mutation, `harness/src/bin/seed_gen.rs:91-105`; no signature, no sighash, no wire).

1. **The SDK column.** Per batch, after `run_svnode`, run `run_engine --engine gosdk|tssdk --corpus <batch>/corpus/seed.jsonl
   --spec <batch>/spec.jsonl --witnesses <batch>/engines` (no `--record`); the campaign copies
   `<batch>/engines/<engine>/seed.jsonl` to `corpus/fuzz/<date>-batch-<n>-<engine>.jsonl` and reads the counts from its
   `manifest.json`: a new step in `tools/fuzz_campaign.sh`, no change to `run_engine` (`harness/src/bin/run_engine.rs:36-38`).
   The spec input exists (the campaign's step 2, `eval/bsvs corpus`, both columns, `tools/fuzz_campaign.sh:134` on `lane/fuzz-campaign`), so the column itself runs as the
   runners stand; the classifier is `harness/rules/<engine>.json`, the batch line gains per-engine
   `agree`/`divergent`/`unexplained`. No new generator. [EST] 1 lane sitting.
2. **A signed-spend family in `gen.rs`** from bsv-fuzz's `p2pkh_sign_flow` shapes and `p2pkh_verify` strata
   (§2 rows 5 and 6): bsv-rs signs (RFC 6979, deterministic) P2PKH and bare CHECKSIG spends across ALL, NONE, SINGLE,
   ANYONECANPAY, the high-bit and undefined base types, SINGLE past the outputs, 1 to 60 inputs, versions 1 and 2;
   then each signed case's four twins (DER bit flip, `s := n - s`, an output bumped, the four DER mutations of M4).
   This reaches CHECKSIG and the sighash through the node, the evaluator and both SDKs at once. [EST] 2 sittings.
3. **The must-reject probes.** bsv-fuzz's idea (`engine_corpus.rs:18-20`, `FINDINGS.md:140,142`): a probe class whose
   answer is known before the run and is counted apart from the agreement count. Here: each generated case that the
   spec accepts gets a deterministic twin the spec's reference model or the signature twin of item 2 says must fail;
   the batch line counts `probes` and `probes_failed` and a probe the node ACCEPTS is a witness under its own class.
   bsv-fuzz's one-directional tally (`engine_corpus.rs:1213-1226`: `native_stricter` safe, `forbidden` fatal) becomes,
   for the SDK column, separate counts of "engine accepts what the spec rejects" and "engine rejects what the spec
   accepts" (a count, never a ruling; rule 4). [EST] 1 sitting.
4. **The wire column** (#86): a batch of `corrupt_beef` mutants (§2 row 7, ported as written with its provenance
   header) over every mainnet row of `corpus/wire/{beef,atomic-beef,ef,bump}.jsonl`, plus a `beef_gen`-shaped builder
   at `@bsv/sdk` 3.0.0 for depth 1 and 2 graphs, run through `wirecheck`, `run_wire`, the go and ts wire adapters and
   `tools/wire_compare.py`; Findings A and B become two degenerate shapes (a value past 2^53 - 1; a short read the
   lenient reader takes). A wire divergence is a view's witness, never a script verdict. [EST] 2 sittings.
5. **The seed and reproduction discipline bsv-fuzz has and the campaign lacks:** (a) a per-class salted seed
   (`SEED ^ salt`, `engine_corpus.rs:628,821,1039,1303`) so a new family never shifts another family's cases, where
   today one PRNG stream picks the family (`seed_gen.rs:92`); (b) a replay-ahead regression file (proptest's
   `*.proptest-regressions`): every witness's smallest case replayed at the head of every batch before fresh cases;
   (c) per-class strata counters on the batch line (bsv-fuzz prints them per class, `engine_corpus.rs:1744-1790`;
   the campaign prints per-class divergences only); (d) the prefix property (`README.md:92-96`): the tripwire's fuzz
   smoke runs a strict prefix of a campaign batch so the smoke and the campaign share cases. What the campaign
   already has and bsv-fuzz lacks: a record per batch (the log line, the sha256 of four binaries, the seed derivation;
   bsv-fuzz's later classes have no `FINDINGS.md` row, §1). [EST] 1 sitting.
6. **What to cite, not port:** `SIGHASH.md` (as a reading of three SDKs' sighash entry points, stale where §3 says),
