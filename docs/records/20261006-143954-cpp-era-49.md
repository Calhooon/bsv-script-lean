## 1. The question, and the answer

The replay of `corpus/cpp` (`docs/records/20261006-050824-cpp-replay-49.md`) had projected the 35,844 `CheckInputs` calls of
`txvalidationcache_tests` at mainnet's heights (600,000 and 700,000) without the coinbase flag, and the merge32 lens (a)
(`docs/records/20261006-054540-merge32-49.md` §4) made them undecided by name: the test's coins stand at height 1, coinbase,
under a tip at 100, on a chain of the test's own, an era other than the one judged. This sitting reproduced that era and judged
them: the fixture's chain rebuilt block for block on the lane's own two instances, each call's coins read from that chain at
their own heights, the node's two activation heights set per call as the test sets them, the spec told the same chain, and the
word the one `CheckInputScripts` passes to `CScriptCheck`.

**Finding.** None. On the 35,844 spends the spec's evaluator and SV-node v1.2.2 agree in both columns (the consensus instance under
the call's word, the policy instance under the relay word of the test's chain), with 0 flag-word drifts and 0 undecided runs; the
node agrees with the C++ assertion (`returns`) on every one of the 35,844 (0 disagreeing, 0 without a deciding assertion); no
witness was filed under `corpus/witnesses/sv-node/` (seven empty files). No Finding of the reference against itself (rule 15).
The spec's pre-Genesis rows carry no claim beyond the node's verdicts on them (`docs/WRITE-UP.md` §5, item 3, which names no
cache row and is unchanged).

  (`rpc/mining.cpp:41–55`; the nonce 1 after its increment, `:50`; the flags empty, `validation.cpp:117`). So block h's coinbase
  is version 2, `<h> <1>`, 50 BTC (regtest's subsidy below its halving at 150, `chainparams.cpp:1460`) to the P2PK, and its txid
  is the fixture's whatever the header's time and nonce: the coinbase at height 1 built so hashes to `a85bb2c772794062…`, the
  outpoint of the test's first spend `[X]`; the double-spend case's recorded `coin_tx` is reproduced byte for byte `[X]`.
- **The calls.** `checkinputs_test` (`txvalidationcache_tests.cpp:300–620`) sets Genesis at 102 (`:329`) and runs
  `ValidateCheckInputsForAllFlags` (`:85–185`) five times: every word below `SCRIPT_FLAG_LAST` (`:98`) that carries the
  mandatory flags (`:112`; `PRE_CHRONICLE_MANDATORY_SCRIPT_VERIFY_FLAGS` = P2SH, STRICTENC, FORKID, NULLFAIL, LOW_S,

  UTXO_AFTER_GENESIS, else to the tip + 2 (`:116–120`), Chronicle likewise (`:122–126`); then `CheckInputs(config, consensus =
  true, tx, tipView, true, test_flags, …)` (`:130–142`). The first loop spends the coinbase at height 1 under a tip at 100

  The corpus recorded each call's heights and the config's two activation heights (`tip_height`, `prevouts[].height`,
  `prevouts[].is_coinbase`, `policy_at_tip_height`).
- **What `CheckInputs` computes.** `validation.cpp:2841–2930`: `Consensus::CheckTxInputs` at the spend height, the tip's
  successor (`GetSpendHeightAndMTP`, `:2858`), then per input `CheckInputScripts(…, coin->GetHeight(), spendHeight, flags, …)`
  (`:2915–2916`), which ORs `InputScriptVerifyFlags(era(spendHeight), era(coinHeight))` into the word `CScriptCheck` receives

  config gives, so the OR adds SIGPUSHONLY alone, on the words run under Genesis at 1.
- **The instrument.** `verifyscript` (`rpc/misc.cpp:509–917`) without `txo` reads the coin's lock, value and HEIGHT from the
