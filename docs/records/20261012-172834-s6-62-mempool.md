## Findings and qualifications (rule 15)

**Finding (rule 15).** The unqualified held-lock conflict law in the task is
false at the pin. Accepted `mapNextTx` locks always conflict once stage 5 is
reached. Non-final locks are consulted only for a final request and exempt
`finalisesExistingTransaction`; non-final updates reach commit's equal-input,
non-decreasing-sequence and increase checks. The preliminary finalisation test
can accept a subset of the held inputs, but commit then rejects unequal counts.
The theorems use exactly these qualifications. [SRC]
`src/txmempool.cpp:2257–2280`, `src/time_locked_mempool.cpp:114–156,516–589` at `6504a3af`.

**Finding (rule 15).** Expire removes descendants of strictly old roots,
including younger descendants. The requested age-only removal statement is
replaced by the exact removal-closure theorem plus the old-root theorem. The
corpus has equality, older-root and younger-descendant witnesses. [SRC]
`src/txmempool.cpp:2201–2220` at `6504a3af`.

**Finding (rule 15).** Trim accepts equality with the size limit and stops when
empty, even if retained `mapDeltas` allocations exceed an artificially small
limit. Its global size law is therefore `usage ≤ limit OR accepted = []`;
the byte-only profile has the unconditional `usage ≤ limit` corollary. The
candidate tracker chooses eligible leaves or group leaves, not an arbitrary
lowest individual fee. The rolling minimum considers both the removed maximum
and the next survivor, adds the census increment, and caps the bump at the
mining fee while secondary entries remain; emptying need not erase a previous
rolling rate. [SRC] `src/txmempool.cpp:1365–1383,2097–2110,2650–2666,2704–2798`,
`src/txmempoolevictioncandidates.cpp:57–118,170–174` at `6504a3af`.

**Finding (rule 15).** The ancestor admission statistic is maximum dependency
path length, not total distinct ancestors. Secondary grouping data can count
shared ancestors repeatedly through distinct parents; the source subsequently
forms a unique CPFP group without re-testing its precise fee aggregate. The
caps reject equality only inside a parent iteration, so a root passes a zero
ancestor cap. [SRC] `src/txmempool.cpp:194–247,370–435,452–565` at `6504a3af`.

**Finding (rule 15).** The non-final update's `finalised` flag is cleared only
by an increased sequence that is not final. An unchanged non-final sequence
can therefore coexist with an update queued as finalised. The corpus's
`held-unchanged-nonfinal-sequence-queues` witnesses the source control flow;
revalidation remains mandatory. [SRC] `src/time_locked_mempool.cpp:516–589,73–80`
at `6504a3af`.

## Stack captain mapping answer

[M] #62's mapping answer: after Genesis, a transaction that is non-final at the admission tip and passes the remaining policy checks is a HOLD, with derived status `heldNonFinal` while its id is in the non-final pool (DESIGN-S6 §3, row 3); `SetNonFinal` does not construct a rejection, and commit routes it to `addOrUpdateTransaction` ([SRC] bitcoin-sv `6504a3aff65ba97c0f6c80962b033e35ecbfed4b`, [validation.cpp:1107–1138](https://github.com/bitcoin-sv/bitcoin-sv/blob/6504a3aff65ba97c0f6c80962b033e35ecbfed4b/src/validation.cpp#L1107-L1138), [912–926](https://github.com/bitcoin-sv/bitcoin-sv/blob/6504a3aff65ba97c0f6c80962b033e35ecbfed4b/src/validation.cpp#L912-L926); [design status](https://github.com/Calgooon/bsv-script-lean/blob/e33d5d4/docs/DESIGN-S6.md#3-the-derived-status-function)). A miner's reader without the tip cannot judge this contextual finality and declines as `Unverifiable(Finality)`; with a tip, bsv-rs 0.5.0's `NonFinal` is the reader's next-block refusal, not a node admission verdict ([SRC] bsv-rs `d06ea0dd12afba8f1a5ced9daf7faa61ba97ea69`, [beef_stream.rs:2168–2193](https://github.com/Calhooon/bsv-rs/blob/d06ea0dd12afba8f1a5ced9daf7faa61ba97ea69/src/transaction/beef_stream.rs#L2168-L2193)). The next block uses its own full-width cutoff and `contextualCheckTransactionForBlock` from #60, so it can refuse block inclusion while admission holds the transaction ([SRC] [validation.cpp:5977–5996,6042–6074](https://github.com/bitcoin-sv/bitcoin-sv/blob/6504a3aff65ba97c0f6c80962b033e35ecbfed4b/src/validation.cpp#L5977-L6074)). Later height or time finality makes the periodic pool check remove the hold and enqueue full revalidation, including the frozen-list checks; only successful revalidation makes the status `inMempool`, and the intermediate queued state need not retain `heldNonFinal` ([SRC] [time_locked_mempool.cpp:611–660](https://github.com/bitcoin-sv/bitcoin-sv/blob/6504a3aff65ba97c0f6c80962b033e35ecbfed4b/src/time_locked_mempool.cpp#L611-L660)). Thus a wallet word reporting this retained node transaction maps to HOLD/`heldNonFinal`; treating a next-block refusal as the node's `rejected` status would be a no-function witness for #67, while reporting it as a contextual reader decline preserves the distinction. Pool-disabled, rate, conflict or later validation refusals remain separate outcomes, not consequences of non-finality alone.
