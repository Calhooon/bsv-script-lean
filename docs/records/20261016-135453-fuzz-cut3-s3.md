## 4. The signature and the saturation stopping rule

**The signature** (`tools/fuzz_batch.py`'s header): every node cell (a spend and a column, `spec` or `spec-mempool`)
has one: the family (the corpus part: smoke, replay or the seed_gen family), the column, the spec's verdict and its halt
reason word (`code`, `-` when none), the node's verdict and its reject reason as the classifier names it (run_svnode's
`node_code`, the reason `harness/src/svnode_codes.rs` maps the node's description to; from run_svnode's new `--cells`
file, one line per cell run, never the description), and the sorted set of distinct opcodes in the spend's scripts
(every input's unlocking script and every prevout's locking script; each opcode as two hex digits, the direct pushes
0x01 to 0x4b as the one token `01-4b`, a push past the script's end as `trunc`) (except a mutation cell's, the [D] below). Text, space-separated, no transaction
bytes. `$FUZZ_STATE/signatures.tsv` holds one line per signature with the batch that saw it earliest, sorted, unique,
outside the repository; the batch line gains `new_sigs=<n> sigs_total=<n>`. The column is in the signature because the
verdicts are per column.

**The rule** (`tools/fuzz_campaign.sh`'s header): the loop stops ITSELF when `new_sigs=0` for `FUZZ_SAT_BATCHES` (20)
consecutive batches at one TUPLE (the campaign tree's commit; the families line with the batch and smoke counts; the
sha256 of the replay rows as they stand, the files of `$FUZZ_REPLAY` in order, which is the input the batch line's
`replay_sha256` is computed from and is readable between batches without composing one; the binaries' sha256 line;
since item 8, the resource set's sha256): it
writes `$FUZZ_STATE/SATURATED` holding the tuple and logs `saturated: <tuple> after batch <n>`; the streak survives a
restart in `$FUZZ_STATE/sat.streak`. While SATURATED names the current tuple the loop sleeps `FUZZ_IDLE_SLEEP` between
checks and runs no batch; the moment the tuple differs it logs `woken: <what moved>` (the fields that differ, old and new
abbreviated) and runs again. The belt: `FUZZ_DAY_MAX` (200) completed batches per UTC day, then idle until the next
day (`day cap` logged once). `--once` runs regardless of both. [D] 20: a million fresh spends without one new signature,
about an hour at the two days' pace; [D] 200: ten million fresh spends a day, some 36 % of the two days' pace of about

## 8. The belt: the spec stage's watchdog and the resource set (lane fix2-fuzz-cut3, 2026-10-16)

Why: `[M]` one legal post-Genesis spend with a 2 GB `OP_NUM2BIN` element costs the evaluator some 30 GB resident (#142:
the block column's stack memory limit is the consensus `INT64_MAX`, the reference builds the element too; the cost is
the element representation, #143, an S1 lane). Until #143 lands the campaign must be unable to take the 36 GB box into
the compressor whatever an entry costs; the owner's cut 3 ruling (2026-10-16 12:5xZ, #141) stands, and this is its belt.
In `tools/fuzz_campaign.sh` (its header names every piece), `tools/fuzz_compose.py` and `tools/fuzz_batch.py`:

- THE WATCHDOG. Every `bsvs` process of the spec stage is the script's child, its resident memory read by `ps -o rss=`
  every `FUZZ_SPEC_POLL` (0.1) s; past `FUZZ_SPEC_RSS_MB` (6144) it gets TERM, then KILL after 5 s, and its piece is
  bisected into `FUZZ_BISECT` (8) pieces, each under the same watchdog, down to the single entries that cost it. A
  killed single entry gets no line in `spec.jsonl`; its id goes to `<batch>/resource.txt` and its corpus line as
  composed is appended to `$FUZZ_STATE/resource/<date>.jsonl` (outside the repository, never printed). `run_svnode`
  and `run_engine` skip an entry without a spec line and count it `without a spec verdict` (`harness/src/bin/run_svnode.rs`
  `no_spec`, `run_engine.rs` the same), and `run_svnode`'s GREEN test does not read that count, so it is counted in
  the batch line's `no_spec` (spends without a spec line; its two cells are neither agreements nor divergences), and the verdict word stays GREEN when nothing diverges. The batch
  line gains `resource=<n>` and `resource_ms=<the wall of the killed runs and the bisect pieces>`; a resource cell's
  signature reads `spec:resource:-`; a witness row replayed on a resource case counts `unrun`. `resource` is the
  campaign's own gap, `[M]` per batch, not the spec's verdict.
- THE RESOURCE REPLAY. Every batch replays the resource set (after the witness replay, before the fresh part, one
  case per spend; a spend already in the batch is flagged on its case), one process each under the watchdog, before
  the chunks; the batch line gains `resource_replay=rows:<n>,cleared:<n>,still:<n>`, counted apart from `replay=`. An
