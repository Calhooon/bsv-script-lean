## 1. What the moves changed in the adapters

- **go-sdk v1.7.0 to v1.7.1** (`go get github.com/bsv-blockchain/go-sdk@v1.7.1`, `go mod tidy`) `[X]`. Within the module, the
  files that differ are `script/interpreter/{engine,options,thread,operations}.go` (and CI files, the CHANGELOG and a stray
  `t3.json`). Two behaviour changes, as the release headings say:
  1. a call that names no era now runs after Chronicle (`thread.go:59–64`; `WithBeforeGenesis` added; `options.go:91–96`:
     `WithFlags` names the era from the word's UTXO bits). The adapter always calls `Execute(WithTx, WithFlags(word))`
     (`harness/engines/go/main.go`), so the default never reaches it: no code change, one comment added.
  2. `opcodeShiftNum` (`operations.go:2563–2605`) changed; among its effects the left shift's size is checked before
     allocating (2593–2596).
  `scriptflag.go`, `errs/error.go`, `config.go`, `number.go`, `stack.go`, `opcodeparser.go` and `transaction/signaturehash.go`
  are byte-identical to v1.7.0 `[X]` (`cmp`/`diff -rq` of the two module-cache copies).
- **@bsv/sdk 2.8.10 to 3.0.0** (`npm install --save-exact @bsv/sdk@3.0.0`, then `npm ci`) `[X]`. The major version's changelog
  heading (ts-stack `packages/sdk/CHANGELOG.md` on main, Unreleased, "Removed (3.0.0 candidate)") removes the legacy
  `identity/DIDTokenValidation` module and its exports. Against `npm pack @bsv/sdk@2.8.10` `[X]`: in `dist/esm`, only
  `identity/` (the removal), `transaction/http/{BinaryFetchClient,DefaultHttpClient}`, `wallet/WalletResultValidation` differ,
  and `primitives/entries/` and `umd.js` are new; `mod.js`, `script/` and the rest of `transaction/` are byte-identical;
  `Spend.js.map` is identical (its `Spend.ts`: 2,321 lines, sha256 `f4337015bc583cdf1c96ebba07940df89e2528a70e54590b2fc8e86a51c099a4`,
  as at 2.8.10). The `exports` map only adds `./primitives/BasePoint`, `./primitives/JacobianPoint` and their `.ts` forms.
  `runner.mjs` imports `Transaction`, `Spend`, `LockingScript` from `@bsv/sdk`, all still exported: **nothing in the
  adapter's interface moved**; its version comments only.
- The source commit of 3.0.0: ts-stack has no 3.0.0 tag and the registry document no `gitHead`. `4d0ea63c85a836e3509291babb0d8e0c784b70a0`
  (2026-10-02T19:48:16Z) is the newest commit touching `packages/sdk/` before the publish (2026-10-03T14:42:41.030Z); its
