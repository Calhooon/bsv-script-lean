  The per-row depth walls are single-row runs (`chaincheck --depth <one row>`, the start-up
  included): `assumevalid` rows 6.86 to 10.16 s, `safemode` 3.64 to 4.20 s, `safemode-rpc` 3.67

- **The operation.** `HeaderTree.get` (`lean/BsvChain/Chain.lean:83`) is a `List.find?` over the

  oracle and the corpus reads are each under 1 % of every mode.
- **In one line:** [D] the `chaincheck` processes are 75.8 s of the 78.8 s sum (96 %), and in each of
  them [M] 97.7 to 99.0 % of the samples are header hashing inside the linear lookup: [D] about 94 % of
  the leg's wall is `HeaderTree.get` recomputing identifiers.

## 5. Proposals (not landed; each changes the semantics' code or a fixture)

1. **P1, S1: stop recomputing identifiers in the header tree's lookups.** [EST] the run of every
   `chaincheck` mode falls by about 95 % (the lookup is 98 to 99 % of it and the comparison
   itself costs nothing beside a double SHA-256); with A, B and C the leg would be bounded by
   `blockcheck` and `oraclecheck` (0.8 and 0.6 s), about 1 to 2 s on the box, and the Studio and
   the `LEAN_NUM_THREADS=4` route would lose their penalty. Two shapes: (a) a `@[csimp]` lemma per
   traversal (`ancestry`, `markInvalid`, `HeaderTree.get`'s callers in the activation loop)
   replacing it in compiled code with a version that hashes each node once per call, proved
   equal to the definition, so no theorem moves and the axiom walk stays clean (`@[csimp]` is
   kernel-checked and is not one of `tools/axiom_check.py`'s refused spellings: the `BYPASS` pattern
   at `tools/axiom_check.py:103-108` does not name it, the declaration scan's self-test reads
   `@[simp] @[csimp] theorem t4` as a theorem at `:571`, and the walk's docstring at
   `lean/AxiomCheck.lean:1269-1270` states that a proven `@[csimp]` replacement is not named); [EST] half a
   day to a day of S1 work per traversal, the proofs being list-induction equalities. (b) the
   identifier stored in `BlockIndex` with its equation as a field; [EST] one to two days, since
   every theorem that unfolds `BlockIndex.id` moves.
2. **P2, S1: `markInvalid` in one pass.** Descendants found by one walk instead of an `ancestry`
   per node: [EST] about 40 to 48 % off the depth, scenarios and status-view runs on its own;
   subsumed by P1 if P1 lands.
3. **Not proposed: a native hash.** An `implemented_by` or `extern` SHA-256 would cut the 40 %
   that is hashing proper, but it is a compiler override, which the axiom walk counts and the
   tripwire refuses: the walk's override census pushes every `implemented_by` and `extern`
   constant (`lean/AxiomCheck.lean:1299-1301`), the judge fails on any count above zero
   (`tools/axiom_check.py:510-511`), and the source scan refuses both spellings (`BYPASS`,
   `implemented_by` and `\bextern\b` at `tools/axiom_check.py:103`); `make axioms` printed "0
   compiler overrides".
4. **Not proposed: one `chaincheck` process for all modes.** After A the start-up is 0.05 s, so
   the saving is under 0.3 s.
