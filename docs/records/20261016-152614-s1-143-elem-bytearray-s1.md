## The interface: `lean/BsvScript/Impl/Elem.lean` (575 lines, 50 theorems, every one new)

`abbrev Elem := ByteArray`. Nothing outside the file calls `ByteArray`'s API; the other Impl files reach an element
through these names, each with its lemma to the list operation through `Elem.toBytes`.

- The conversions: `toBytes` (the bytes as `Nat`, in order), `ofBytes` (a value at or above 256 kept modulo 256, said in
  its docstring), `validB` (`Bytes.valid` as a test, `validB_iff`). `toBytes_valid` (an element's bytes are bytes),
  `toBytes_ofBytes` (the round trip under `Bytes.valid`), `ofBytes_toBytes` (unconditional), `toBytes_inj`. Their
  compiled forms are two loops, `@[csimp] toBytes_eq_fast` and `@[csimp] ofBytes_eq_fast` (`toBytesLoop`,
  `pushList`, `data_pushList`).
- Size, emptiness, equality: `length_toBytes` (the size in one read), `empty` with `toBytes_empty`,
  `isEmpty_toBytes`, `decide_toBytes_eq` (`OP_EQUAL` compares the packed arrays, the runtime's
  `lean_sarray_dec_eq`), `head?` with `head?_toBytes` (`OP_IF`'s minimal-if test).
- Append, take, drop: `toBytes_append` (`OP_CAT`), `take`/`drop` (`ByteArray.extract`) with `toBytes_take`,
  `toBytes_drop` (`OP_SPLIT`, `OP_LEFT`, `OP_RIGHT`, `OP_SUBSTR`).
- The constants and the encodings: `encode` with `toBytes_encode`, `vchTrue` with `toBytes_vchTrue`,
  `toBytes_vchFalse`, `versionLE` with `versionLE_valid` and `toBytes_versionLE`, `minimallyEncode` with
  `toBytes_minimallyEncode`.
- The number readers, the length bound read from the size before any list is built: `readOperand`,
  `readInt64Operand`, `readCount`, `readNum`, each with its `_eq` lemma to the spec's reader on the element's list.
  `castToBool` with `castToBool_eq`.
- `OP_NUM2BIN`'s pad, written in place into one array of the width's capacity: `pushZeros` (the replicate, one
  `ByteArray.push` each) with `toBytes_pushZeros`, `pushList` with `toBytes_pushList`, `num2binPad` (the top byte's
  sign bit cleared, the zeros, the sign byte: the set-last of the issue's list is this cleared top byte and the pushed
  sign byte) with `toBytes_num2binPad`, and `num2binM` (the padding step on the operand's minimal encoding, computed
  once by the opcode) with `num2binM_map`.
- The bitwise family: `zipWith` with `toBytes_zipWith` and `bitwise_byte`, `invert` with `toBytes_invert`, `zeros`
  with `toBytes_zeros`, `lshift`/`rshift` with `toBytes_lshift`/`toBytes_rshift`, `lshift_valid`/`rshift_valid`,
  `or_byte`, `and_byte`, `lmask_lt`, `rmask_lt`.
- The hash inputs: `hashOp` with `toBytes_hashOp`; `sha256_valid`, `sha1_valid`, `ripemd160_valid`,
  `flatten_wordBytes_valid`, `flatten_wordBytesLE_valid` (a digest is bytes, so its packing is exact).

What is still read through a list: the truth tests (`Elem.castToBool` is `castToBool` on `toBytes`), the operand of
`OP_BIN2NUM` and of `OP_NUM2BIN` (its minimal encoding, `Impl/Splice.lean:71`, `:114`), the final top the verdict reads
(`implView.top?`), the number
readers once the size admits the operand, the signature and key checks and the scriptCode's cleanup, the hashes,
`OP_AND`/`OP_OR`/`OP_XOR`, `OP_INVERT` and the shifts. Each is exact and costs one list of the element's length; none
of them is a 2 GB element's path in the two entries below. Packed natively: the stack, the pushes and pops and their
memory charges, `OP_CAT`, `OP_SPLIT`, `OP_SUBSTR`/`OP_LEFT`/`OP_RIGHT`, `OP_SIZE`, `OP_EQUAL`, `OP_NUM2BIN`'s pad and
the zeros of the shifts.
