## Findings

- **The issue's ban sentence.** "A ban is reached only through recorded misbehavior increments" is false of the ban
  list at the pin: a bad message start, an oversized or malformed header and a stream moved across addresses ban
  directly, as does `setban`. `ban_sound_run` states the true form (a ban for misbehavior needs recorded
  increments at the threshold or more), `ban_without_increment` the witness by evaluation, step s8 the witness on a
  node.
- **revokemid (rule 15).** `allNetMessageTypes` omits `revokemid` against its own comment
  (`src/protocol.cpp:103-121`); `registered_omits_revokemid`.
- **Teranode, reading only.** `services/p2p` carries no transaction inventory; its score decays (1 point a minute)
  and `services/legacy`'s halves every 60 s and bans only strictly above the threshold, so `ban_sound_run` does not
  transfer (docs/CONFORMANCE-P2P-RELAY.md section 5). The measured leg waits on #66's cut 2.
