## Walls and limits

- The model takes `peer_id`'s order as data (`peerRank`); the collation (SQLite BINARY, PostgreSQL's) is not modeled.
- The mark's partial failures (a batch error leaving some records unwritten) are not modeled; a missing record stops
  the store (`none`), as the source's `Fatalf` stops the process.
- The assembler's reorg sets (`moveBackBlocks`, `moveForwardBlocks`, the losing conflicts and the whole pool folded
  into the mark-false set, `SubtreeProcessor.go:4374-4452`) are read as "the transactions of the blocks that leave or
  join the best row's ancestry"; the assembler's own state is #92's and #90's.
- `reconsider`'s snapshot orders equal heights by insertion; the database's order among them is unspecified.
- The bridge theorems take the correspondence as a hypothesis: #88 constructs it.
