## The statements [M]

1. `scriptCode_unlock_site` (`Sighash.lean:46`): in the two environments `verifyScript` builds from `params.flags`
   (`mkEnv params ctx params.flags.forkAdjust (some scriptPubKey)` for the unlocking phase, `... none` for the locking
   phase), `scriptCodeOf` is `st.codeSep ++ scriptPubKey` in the unlocking phase with `params.flags.chronicle`,
   `st.codeSep` in the unlocking phase without it, and `st.codeSep` in the locking phase. [SRC] `1471-1481`, `2338-2342`.
   Its two companions: `codeSep_sites` (`:68`): `evalScript` starts the loop with `codeSep` the whole script (within
   `maxScriptSize`), an executed `OP_CODESEPARATOR` sets it to the bytes after itself, an unexecuted one changes
   nothing (`407`, `1445-1448`, `482`); `scriptCode_unlock_site_forkid` (`:84`): with FORKID and CHRONICLE in the word
   and a signature carrying the FORKID bit, `cleanupScriptCode (scriptCodeOf env st) sig forkid`, the bytes
   `checkSigOp` hands its checker, is `st.codeSep ++ scriptPubKey` (`1484`, `255-263`).
2. `digest_dispatch_chronicle` (`:106`): `hasChronicle t` gives `sighash ... e = sighashOriginal ...` for every `e`;
   `hasForkId t` and not `hasChronicle t` give `sighash ... true = sighashBIP143 ...`. [SRC] `2112-2124`.
3. `single_past_outputs` (`:125`): with `t.baseType = SIGHASH_SINGLE` and `tx.outputs.length ≤ nIn`:
   `sighashOriginal ... = one256`; `sighash ... e = one256` for a type with the CHRONICLE bit and every `e`;
   `bip143Outputs tx nIn t = zero32`; `sighashBIP143` is SHA-256d of the preimage with `zero32` in the `hashOutputs`
   field. [SRC] `2086-2100`; `2034-2083`.

## What is not stated as asked [D]

"From its last executed OP_CODESEPARATOR" is stated through `codeSep` and its two writing sites (`codeSep_sites`), not
as one invariant over a whole run: the loop takes the opcode table as a parameter (`OpExec`), and a statement that no
other opcode writes `codeSep` would be a theorem over every family's implementation. The opcodes other than the
separator do not write it by their definitions; the run-level sentence is not proved here.
